CHANGES IMPLEMENTED
===================

1. RACE DISPLAY ON ALL EMBEDS
   Location: buildCardEmbed(), buildDropEmbed(), buildUserCardEmbed() in lib/cardEmbed.js
   
   - Race is now displayed at the TOP of the stats section
   - Shows as: **Race:** [Race Name]
   - Appears on:
     * Pull embeds (buildCardEmbed)
     * Drop embeds (buildDropEmbed)
     * User inventory embeds (buildUserCardEmbed)


2. SPECIAL CONDITION DISPLAY NEXT TO SPECIAL ATTACKS
   Location: buildCardEmbed(), buildDropEmbed(), buildUserCardEmbed() in lib/cardEmbed.js
   
   - Special Condition now shows next to Special Attack name
   - Format: **Special:** [Attack Name] ([damage range]) [Special Condition]
   - Example: **Special:** Gum Gum pistol (25-40 damage) [Stun]
   - Only displays if specialCondition field exists on the card
   - Appears on all card embeds


3. RACE BOOSTS IN USER STATS BOOSTS FIELD
   Location: buildUserCardEmbed() in lib/cardEmbed.js
   
   - Race boosts now calculated automatically
   - Displayed in **Boosts:** field alongside other boosts
   - Format: Race: ATK +[amount] and Race: HP +[amount]
   - Calculated as percentage of current stats
   - Works alongside weapon boosts and team boosts
   - Example: **Boosts:** ATK +5% • HP +10% • Race: ATK +25 • Race: HP +50


4. RACE BOOST SYSTEM (lib/stats.js)
   Location: lib/stats.js
   
   - Added raceBoosts object with 16 race types:
     * Human: +5% all stats
     * Fish-Man: +15% Power, +10% Health, -5% Speed
     * Merfolk: +20% Speed, +5% Health, -10% Power
     * Giant: +30% Health, +20% Power, -20% Speed
     * Buccaneer: +25% Health, +15% Power, 10% damage reduction
     * Skypiean: +15% Speed, +5% Power, +10% evasion
     * Shandian: +15% Power, +10% Speed, +5% crit chance
     * Birkan: +20% Special Attack effectiveness, -5% Health
     * Longarm Tribe: +10% Power, +10% hit chance, +5% SC chance
     * Longleg Tribe: +25% Speed, +10% first strike, -5% Health
     * Mink: +15% Speed, +10% Power, +20% Electro boost
     * Tontatta (Dwarf): +30% Speed, +20% evasion, -20% Health
     * Lunarian: +20% Health, +15% Power, 15% damage reduction
     * Three-Eye Tribe: +15% Special Attack, +10% accuracy, +5% ignore buffs
     * Cyborg: +20% Power, +10% Health, Poison immunity
     * Seraphim: +25% Health, +15% Power, -10% Speed
   
   - Added getRaceBoosts(race) function
   - Returns boost values for stat calculations
